Support and help

Contact us

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Write to help@bbjg.app. We usually reply within a few business days.

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description (a screenshot helps).

Restore purchases

If you bought something and it is missing, for example after reinstalling the game or switching to a new iPhone, open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID that made the purchase.

Refunds and billing

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
